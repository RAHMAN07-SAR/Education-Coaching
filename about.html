About Us

Our journey — empowering learners to reach their potential

Based in Bengaluru, EduCoach (MasterLife) combines evidence-based learning strategies, supportive coaching, and industry-informed curriculum to help students transform their careers and lives. Our methodology balances mindset, skill-building, and practical experience.

Our Story

EduCoach started as a small study group in Bengaluru and evolved into a structured coaching institute with a mission to democratize access to high-quality learning. Over the last 9+ years we have worked with students from diverse backgrounds — helping them prepare for exams, build careers, and develop life skills that matter.

Human-first approach

We put learners first: individual learning plans and coaching.

Evidence-based

Teaching methods are backed by research and iterative feedback.
